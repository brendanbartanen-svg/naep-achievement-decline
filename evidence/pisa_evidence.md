## International decline
- OECD avg 2018→2022: math −15 (record; no prior consecutive-cycle change exceeded 4), reading −10 (2x prior record). 20 pts ≈ 1 year of schooling.
- OECD avg 2012→2022: math 494→472 (−22); reading 496→476 (−20).
- OECD press release: decline "only partly attributable to COVID" — reading/science falling for about a decade pre-2018. https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2023/12/decline-in-educational-performance-only-partly-attributable-to-the-covid-19-pandemic.html
- Pre-2018 math decliners: Australia, Belgium, Canada, Czech, Finland, Hungary, Iceland, Korea, Netherlands, NZ, Slovakia, Switzerland (OECD Vol I).
- 2018→2022 math: Iceland −36, Norway −33, Netherlands −27, Poland −27, Germany −25, Finland −23, Sweden −21, France −21, US −13 (to 465). Gainers: Japan +9, Taipei +16, Singapore +6.
- US reading held ~flat (505→504) 2018→2022 while OECD fell — US relative position IMPROVED.

## Smartphones / screens
- Pew teen smartphone ownership/access: 23% (2011) → 37% (2012) → 73% (2014-15) → 95% (2018, 45% online "almost constantly") → 95% (2023).
- Common Sense Media entertainment screen time: teens 6h40m (2015) → 7h22m (2019) → 8h39m (2021); tweens 4h36m → 4h44m → 5h33m.
- PISA 2022: OECD avg 65% distracted by own device in some math lessons (US: 66%); distracted-most-lessons students score 15 pts lower; ≤1hr/day leisure device use at school vs 5-7hr = 49 pts higher math (SES-adjusted); notifications-off → less distraction. OECD: cross-sectional, not causal. https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-ii_a97db61c-en.html

## Caveats
- OECD-average membership changes across cycles; PISA 2022 delayed a year; PISA 2006 US reading missing; distraction data cross-sectional.

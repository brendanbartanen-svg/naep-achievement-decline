## Implications for the report (v1.9 scope if/when updated)
1. **Verdicts unchanged, strengthened.** Phase-1 numbers (2012→2020) untouched. The
   age-13 continued P10 fall + top recovery extends the main-NAEP "top recovers, bottom
   keeps falling" finding into LTT through fall 2024.
2. **One claim now needs nuance:** "no aggregate recovery since" — true at age 13, no
   longer at age 9 (+3.8/+3.8, bottom-led). First genuinely positive signal in the dataset.
   Tension to handle: main-NAEP 2024 G4 showed bottom still falling; LTT age-9 2025 (a year
   later, age- not grade-based) shows bottom-led recovery — discuss timing, don't paper over.
3. **Cohort reading consistent with the synthesis:** the recovering age-9 cohort (b. ~2015-16)
   had little pandemic-era schooling; the stuck age-13 cohort (b. ~2011-12) is the COVID-G4
   cohort now in adolescence — period effect in adolescence persists.
4. Files to touch: `data/ltt.csv` (+4 rows), `analyze_ltt.py` (+hard-coded percentile arrays
   if any), `figures/` LTT figures, `evidence/ltt_evidence.md`, report §LTT/phase-structure
   text + abstract "no recovery" sentence, brief if it cites LTT, `checks.py` (add 2025
   frozen assertions; existing year-indexed checks unaffected), STATE/README headline lines.

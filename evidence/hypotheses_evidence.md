# Evidence File: Hypotheses for the US Student-Achievement Decline
(gathered by research agent, June 2026 — each fact has source URL)

## 1. COVID school closures / remote instruction
- Goldhaber, Kane, McEachin, Morton, Patterson & Staiger (AER: Insights 5(3): 377-92, 2023; NBER w30010; CALDER WP 267-0522). NWEA MAP, 2.1M students, ~10,000 schools. Districts remote >50% of 2020-21: math growth shortfall 0.44 SD in high-poverty schools vs 0.26 SD low-poverty (gap +0.18 SD). Where in-person, both groups lost ~0.17 SD and math gaps did NOT widen. "Remote instruction was a primary driver of widening gaps."
  https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Faeri.20220180 ; https://www.nber.org/papers/w30010
- Jack, Halloran, Okun & Oster (AER: Insights 2023; NBER w29497). 12 states, spring 2021 pass rates. Avg math pass-rate decline 14.2pp; only 4.1pp in fully in-person districts. ELA 6.3pp vs 3.1pp. In-person ≈ 13-14pp higher math pass rates than fully virtual. https://www.nber.org/papers/w29497
- Education Recovery Scorecard (Kane/CEPR + Reardon/Stanford):
  - Jan 2024: 2019-22 losses 0.53 grade-equivalents math, 0.31 reading; 2022-23 recovery 0.17 GE math (~1/3), 0.08 reading (~1/4). Affluent-poor math gap +11% since 2019. Absenteeism normalization would have added 0.03-0.05 GE. https://educationrecoveryscorecard.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/ERS-Report-Final-1.31.pdf
  - Feb 2025 (8,719 districts, 43 states): only 17% of students in districts at/above 2019 math, 11% reading, 6% both. Top-income-decile districts ~4x more likely recovered than bottom decile (14.1% vs 3.9%). High-poverty recovery driven by federal relief. https://educationrecoveryscorecard.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Pivoting-from-Pandemic-Recovery-to-Long-Term-Reform-A-District-Level-Analysis.pdf
  - May 2026: "U-shaped recovery" (best in highest- and lowest-income districts); pre-COVID slide: 2015-2025, 83% of districts lost ground in reading; 1 in 3 behind by a full grade level. https://www.gse.harvard.edu/ideas/news/26/05/new-education-scorecard-finds-u-shaped-recovery

## 2. Chronic absenteeism
- AEI Return to Learn Tracker (Malkus): ~15% (2017-19) → ~28.5-29% (2021-22) → 25.4% (2022-23) → 23.5% (2023-24); still ~8-9pp above pre-pandemic. https://www.returntolearntracker.net/ ; https://www.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Lingering-Absence-in-Public-Schools-Tracking-Post-Pandemic-Chronic-Absenteeism-into-2024.pdf
- CEA (Sept 2023): absenteeism accounts for ~27% of 2019-22 NAEP G4 math decline and ~45% of reading decline. https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/cea/written-materials/2023/09/13/chronic-absenteeism-and-disrupted-learning-require-an-all-hands-on-deck-approach/
- NAGB: 8th graders missing 3+ days in month before 2015 NAEP scored 0.3-0.6 SD lower. https://www.nagb.gov/naep/chronic-absenteeism.html
- AEI MD/NC study: 10 absences → math achievement −5-6% of SD. https://www.aei.org/research-products/report/absences-and-achievement-after-the-pandemic-evidence-from-maryland-and-north-carolina/

## 3. Accountability policy
- Timeline: first 10 NCLB waivers Feb 9, 2012; 33 states + DC by Sept 2012. ESSA signed Dec 10, 2015; full effect 2017-18. https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/blog/2012/02/09/everything-you-need-know-waivers-flexibility-and-reforming-no-child-left-behind ; https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/the-every-student-succeeds-act-an-essa-overview/2016/03
- Dee & Jacob (JPAM 2011): NCLB raised G4 NAEP math +7.2 pts (~0.23 SD) by 2007; G8 math gains concentrated among low achievers, Black/Hispanic, subsidized-lunch; no effect G4 reading. https://tom-dee.github.io/files/jpam2011.pdf
- Aldeman: waiver-era relaxation "set off a decline... across the country"; declines worst among low performers. https://www.americanexperiment.org/how-relaxing-school-accountability-has-hurt-academic-achievement/ ; https://www.chadaldeman.com/p/naep-winners-and-losers
- LTT corroboration: age-13 scores rose until ~2012 then fell; 2023 LTT −7 reading / −14 math vs decade earlier; math at 1992 level, reading at 1971 level; White-Black gaps 23/29 (2012) → 27/42 (2023). https://www.nationsreportcard.gov/highlights/ltt/2023/

## 4. School spending
- NCES: real per-pupil current expenditure bottomed 2012-13 ($13,952 constant); +13% from 2010-11 to 2020-21 ($16,280). https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/cmb/public-school-expenditure
- CBPP: 29 states cut per-pupil funding 2008-2016; 25 states not recovered by 2016; 12 below 2007 by 2019. https://www.cbpp.org/research/state-budget-and-tax/a-punishing-decade-for-school-funding
- Jackson, Wigger & Xiong (NBER w24203): recession spending cuts reduced scores & college-going. https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24203/w24203.pdf
- Jackson & Mackevicius (AEJ:Applied 2024 meta): $1,000/pupil sustained 4 yrs → +0.032-0.035 SD. https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Fapp.20220279
- ESSER: $190B total; two 2024 studies (Goldhaber/Dewey; Kane/Reardon) ≈ +0.008 SD math per $1,000/student; full recovery price ~$904B. https://www.chalkbeat.org/2024/06/26/federal-covid-relief-money-improved-student-achievement-studies-find/

## 5. Demographics
- Hispanic share: 24% (2012) → 29% (2022). https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/cge/racial-ethnic-enrollment
- EL: 9.4% (2011) → 10.6% (2021). https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/cgf/english-learners
- SWD: ~13% early 2010s → 14.7% (2021-22). https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/cgg/students-with-disabilities
- SPM child poverty record-low 5.2% in 2021 (expanded CTC), rose sharply 2022. https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2022/09/record-drop-in-child-poverty.html
- Composition shifts gradual (few pp/decade) vs LTT declines 7-14 pts/decade; declines occur within districts at all income levels (ERS 2026: 83% of districts lost ground in reading 2015-2025).

## 6. Other
- Mississippi: 49th (2013) → 29th (2019) NAEP G4 reading; top 2013-2024 growth nationally; causal SDID study finds 2013 LBPA improved G4 reading & math; caveats: retention-driven selection in G4, smaller G8 effect. https://www.pbs.org/newshour/education/kids-reading-scores-have-soared-in-mississippi-miracle ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S027277572400092X
- Teacher vacancies: ≥36,000 vacant + ≥163,000 underqualified (Nguyen et al. AERA Open 2024); later ~411,000 vacant or under-certified (~1 in 8). https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/23328584241276512
- Grade inflation: HS GPA 3.17 (2010) → 3.36 (2021) while ACT fell 20.8 → 20.3; larger in high-poverty schools (ACT 2022). 2019 HSTS: GPAs up, 12th-grade NAEP math down. https://www.act.org/content/dam/act/unsecured/documents/2022/R2134-Grade-Inflation-Continues-to-Grow-in-the-Past-Decade-Final-Accessible.pdf

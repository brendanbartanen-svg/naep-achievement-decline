# Effort + phone-ban evidence (agent dossier, June 2026) — key numbers

## Effort levels (incentive experiments)
- Gneezy et al. AER:Insights 2019: surprise $25 incentive raised US 10th-grade scores +0.24-0.28 SD; Shanghai null (-0.09); implied PISA +22-24 pts if extrapolated.
- Braun/Kirsch/Yamamoto 2011 TCR: monetary incentives raised G12 NAEP-reading-style scores ≥5 NAEP pts.
- Zamarro et al. JHC 2019: effort proxies explain 32-38% of cross-country PISA variation.
- Balart/Oosterveen/Webbink EER 2018: within-test decline = noncognitive component, predicts growth.
- Akyol/Krishna/Wang: PISA effort bias large, PISA corrects ~half.
- Wise rapid-guessing lit; Avvisati et al. OECD WP 2024 (PISA 2018): rapid guessing 7.4% of students but ~1.6% of responses; country prevalence uncorrelated with mean performance (r in [-0.2,0.2]).

## Effort TRENDS (the confounder question)
- OECD PISA 2022 Vol I Annex A8: thermometer effort -0.2 (OECD avg) 2018→2022; "if it counted" -0.1; 71% report less effort than if graded. [Matches our microdata: US -0.13, OECD -0.16.]
- OECD behavioral checks 2018→2022: first-vs-second-hour gap NARROWED ("performance lower throughout but more so at the beginning"); straight-lining FELL (1.4%→1.2%); OECD conclusion: administration conditions "remained similar"; Albania the exception.
- Caution: Δ(if-counted effort) correlates 0.64 with Δmath across 69 countries — effort and performance co-move (common-cause disengagement plausible).
- NAEP: digital since 2017 (mode change validated, Jewsbury et al. 2019); NO published evidence of rising rapid-guessing/omission 2017-2024; NCES attributes declines to absenteeism etc.
- PIAAC 2023 caveats: US response rate 28%, tablet-only; NCES NRBA concluded procedures reduced bias "however readers should be aware of the potential."
- Counter-evidence to effort-as-explanation: SEDA state accountability tests (Dewey et al. 2026, 70M students) show same 2013-2019 decline; distributional signature (top spared); bottom-heavy pattern recurs among adults.

## Phone bans (causal)
- Beland & Murphy, Labour Economics 2016 (91 English schools, staggered bans, student FE): +6.4% SD average; +14.2% SD for low achievers; null for high achievers.
- Abrahamsson 2024 (Norway, staggered middle-school bans, registers): girls GPA +0.08 SD; bullying -0.42/-0.39 SD; girls' psych GP visits down; largest for low-SES girls; boys academic null.
- Beneito & Vicente-Chirivella, AEA 2022 (Spain, Galicia/C-LM 2015 bans, synth control): math +0.6-0.8 years of learning; bullying down.
- Kessel et al. EER 2020 (Sweden): null — teachers could already confiscate (dose/bindingness matters).
- Figlio & Özek NBER w34388 (Oct 2025, Florida): test-score improvements in year 2; unexcused absences down; short-run suspension spike fades; larger in middle/high school.
- Netherlands Jan-2024 ban: Kohnstamm Instituut 2025 (survey, non-causal): 75% of schools report improved focus.
- UNESCO: 40% of systems had bans in law/policy by end-2024.

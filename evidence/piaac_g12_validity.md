# PIAAC, Grade 12 NAEP, and validity data (agent-verified, June 2026)

## PIAAC adult skills (US, 16-65)
- Literacy: 2017: 271 → 2023: 258 (official −12; trend-comparable chart series 275→272→263). Numeracy: 255 → 249 (−6). NCES caution: methodology differences between cycles; trend series excludes doorstep-interview cases.
- At/below Level 1: literacy 19% (2017) → 28% (2023); numeracy 29% → 34%. Level 4/5 share stable (lit 13%).
- OECD: literacy declined in US + 18 other OECD countries 2012→2023; only Finland & Denmark improved. "Lowest-performing 10% declined... top 10% improved, widening skills inequalities."
- US 16-24 in 2023: lit 261, num 248 — below OECD avg for the age group.
- Sources: https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/piaac/2023/national_results.asp ; https://ies.ed.gov/learn/press-release/u-s-adults-score-par-international-average-literacy-skills-below-international-average-numeracy-and ; OECD US country note https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/survey-of-adults-skills-2023-country-notes_ab4f6b8c-en/united-states_427d6aac-en.html

## Grade 12 NAEP (2024 results released Sept 9, 2025)
- Math mean (0-300): 2005: 150.0 | 2009: 153.3 | 2013: 153.5 (peak) | 2015: 151.5 | 2019: 150.3 | 2024: 146.9 (lowest ever). 2019→2024 percentiles: P10 −5, P25 −5, P50 −4, P75 −3, P90 n.s.
- Reading mean (0-500): 1992: 292.1 | 2013: 288.4 | 2015: 287.0 | 2019: 285.5 | 2024: 282.6 (lowest ever). 1992→2024: P10 −24, P25 −15, P50 −8, P75 −2.
- Caveat: 2024 G12 weighted student participation 68%.
- Sources: https://www.nationsreportcard.gov/reports/mathematics/2024/g12/national-trends/ ; https://www.nationsreportcard.gov/reports/reading/2024/g12/national-trends/

## NAEP validity (exclusion/participation), G4/G8
- Exclusion (share of ALL students): 1-3% in every year 2013-2024, max change ±1pt; SD/EL identification ROSE (G4 22%→28%) while exclusion stayed flat.
- Student participation: 92% (G4), 89% (G8) in BOTH 2022 and 2024; school participation 95 ≥ 85% standard; public school participation 100%.
- 2024 private-school results suppressed (45%/34% participation); Catholic met standards.
- NAEP publishes research-only Full Population Estimates; a 2024 FPE special analysis of exclusion and score trends exists.
- Sources: 2024 technical appendices (reading/math national PDFs on nationsreportcard.gov); https://nces.ed.gov/nationsreportcard/about/fpe.aspx
